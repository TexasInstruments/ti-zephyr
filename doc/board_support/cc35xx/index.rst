Board Support
#############

Overview
********

CC35XX is a family of TI SimpleLink wireless MCUs. The CC35XXE is an Arm
Cortex-M33 based device with Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) connectivity.

Supported Boards
****************

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 40 20

   * - Board ID
     - Device
     - CPU
     - Product page
   * - ``lp_em_cc35x1``
     - CC35XXE
     - Arm Cortex-M33
     - `CC35XXE <https://www.ti.com/product/CC35XXE>`_
