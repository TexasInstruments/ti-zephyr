Board Support
#############

Overview
********

CC23 is a family of TI SimpleLink wireless MCUs. The CC2340R5 is an Arm
Cortex-M0+ based device with an integrated 2.4 GHz transceiver supporting
Bluetooth® Low Energy and IEEE® 802.15.4.

Supported Boards
****************

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 40 20

   * - Board ID
     - Device
     - CPU
     - Product page
   * - ``lp_em_cc2340r53``
     - CC2340R53
     - Arm Cortex-M0+ @ 48 MHz
     - `CC2340R5 <https://www.ti.com/product/CC2340R5>`_
   * - ``lp_em_cc2340r53_wcsp``
     - CC2340R53 (WCSP)
     - Arm Cortex-M0+ @ 48 MHz
     - `CC2340R5 <https://www.ti.com/product/CC2340R5>`_
   * - ``lp_em_cc2340r5moda``
     - CC2340R5MODA (module)
     - Arm Cortex-M0+ @ 48 MHz
     - `CC2340R5 <https://www.ti.com/product/CC2340R5>`_
