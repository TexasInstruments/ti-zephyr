TI EQEP Frequency Measurement

Overview

Measures the frequency of a pulse train with the EQEP frequency-register (fr) method (a fixed unit-timer window). A software timer toggles a GPIO to produce a 100 Hz signal at 25% duty. EQEP0 counts rising edges on phase A, and the change in position over each 1 second window is the measured frequency in hertz.

The position counter runs from 0 to 999 and then restarts, so it wraps every few windows. Readings within 1 Hz of 100 Hz are in tolerance: the software-timed edges can gain or lose one count per window. A reading outside that range is marked [out of tolerance].

Behaviour

  1. Signal. signal-gpios is driven high for one step and low for three steps, at 400 steps per second (100 Hz, 25% duty).

  2. Decoder. EQEP0 is configured for up-count on rising edges only, with the position counter resetting at its maximum value. The unit-timer alarm is re-armed on every expiry so the windows stay back to back.

  3. Report. Each completed window prints the latched position and the position delta. A delta that crosses the maximum is printed with (counter wrapped).

Requirements

One jumper wire from the generated signal to the EQEP phase-A pin. No external encoder is required.

Wiring

On lp_am13e230, connect BoosterPack header J4 pin 40 (PB3) to J2 pin 11 (PB11). PB3 is the GPIO that generates the pulse train. PB11 (GPIO43) is routed through Input X-BAR channel 16 (INPUTXBAR17) to EQEP0A.

Supported Boards

  • lp_am13e230

Building and Running

west build -p always -b lp_am13e230 samples/drivers/counter/eqep_freq_measurement
west flash

Sample Output

TI EQEP frequency measurement sample
eQEP clock 200000000 Hz; position top 999
Signal: 100 Hz, 25% duty
position  100, measured 100 Hz
position  200, measured 100 Hz
...
position    0, measured 100 Hz (counter wrapped)
