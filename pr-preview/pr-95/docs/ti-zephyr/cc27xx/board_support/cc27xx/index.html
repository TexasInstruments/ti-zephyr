Board Support

Overview

CC27XX is a family of TI SimpleLink wireless MCUs. The CC2745 and CC2755 devices are Arm Cortex-M33 based, with an integrated 2.4 GHz transceiver supporting Bluetooth® Low Energy and IEEE® 802.15.4. The CC2755 devices additionally support Matter, Thread and Zigbee.
