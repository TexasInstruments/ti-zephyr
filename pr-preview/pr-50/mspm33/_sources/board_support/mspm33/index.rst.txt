Board Support
#############

Overview
********

MSPM33 is the TI family of Arm Cortex-M33 based microcontrollers with
TrustZone security and enhanced digital peripherals.

Supported Boards
****************

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 40 20

   * - Board ID
     - Device
     - CPU
     - Product page
   * - ``lp_mspm33c321a``
     - MSPM33C321
     - Single-core Arm Cortex-M33 @ 160 MHz
     - `MSPM33C321 <https://www.ti.com/product/MSPM33C321A>`_
