Board Support
#############

Overview
********

MSPM0 is the TI family of Arm Cortex-M0+ based microcontrollers with advanced
analog integration and mixed-signal peripherals. The MSPM0L devices focus on
ultra-low-power operation.

Supported Boards
****************

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 40 20

   * - Board ID
     - Device
     - CPU
     - Product page
   * - ``lp_mspm0g3507``
     - MSPM0G3507
     - Single-core Arm Cortex-M0+ @ 80 MHz
     - `LP-MSPM0G3507 <https://www.ti.com/tool/LP-MSPM0G3507>`_
   * - ``lp_mspm0g3519``
     - MSPM0G3519
     - Single-core Arm Cortex-M0+ @ 80 MHz
     - `LP-MSPM0G3519 <https://www.ti.com/tool/LP-MSPM0G3519>`_
   * - ``lp_mspm0l2228``
     - MSPM0L2228
     - Arm Cortex-M0+ @ 32 MHz
     - `LP-MSPM0L2228 <https://www.ti.com/tool/LP-MSPM0L2228>`_
